# Pharmacy_-Management-System
For Market Shopping
In this project We add Medicine Name,Brand/Type,Category/Batch-No/Preparation,expiry date,Purchase Price,Selling Price, Stock quantity of Midicine. We also Edit and Delete Medicine.
We also Create Invoice of Medicine & also Edit and Delete it as well as we also share and save the invoice.
We also Manage the Categories of Medicines.
We also Manage the supplier for suppliy.
We also see the Sales for specific date.
We also manage the stock
